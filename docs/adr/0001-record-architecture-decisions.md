# 1. Record Architecture Decisions

**Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

AgriCore needs a durable record of architectural choices for portfolio review and long-term maintenance.

## Decision

Use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) under `docs/adr/` with sequential numbering, append-only lifecycle, and Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded.

## Consequences

- Positive: decisions are searchable and reviewable
- Negative: small documentation overhead
- Neutral: template in `docs/adr/template.md`
